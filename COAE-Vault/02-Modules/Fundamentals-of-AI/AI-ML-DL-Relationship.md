# AI, ML, and DL — Relationship

## Summary

AI, ML, and DL are concentric fields, not synonyms. **AI** is the broadest — any system performing tasks that require human-like intelligence. **ML** is a subfield of AI that learns from data without explicit programming. **DL** is a subfield of ML that uses multi-layer neural networks to learn from complex, unstructured data.

```
AI ⊃ ML ⊃ DL
```

## Artificial Intelligence (AI)

Broad field focused on developing intelligent systems capable of tasks that typically require human intelligence: understanding natural language, recognizing objects, making decisions, solving problems, and learning from experience.

**Key areas of AI:**

- **Natural Language Processing (NLP):** enabling computers to understand, interpret, and generate human language
- **Computer Vision:** allowing computers to "see" and interpret images and videos
- **Robotics:** developing robots that perform tasks autonomously or with human guidance
- **Expert Systems:** mimicking the decision-making abilities of human experts

**Primary goal:** augment human capabilities, not just replace human efforts — enhance decision-making and productivity in complex data analysis, prediction, and mechanical tasks.

**Domain applications:**

| Domain | Application |
|--------|-------------|
| Healthcare | Disease diagnosis, drug discovery |
| Finance | Fraud detection, investment optimization |
| Cybersecurity | Threat identification and mitigation |

## How They Work Together

- **Computer Vision:** supervised learning + deep CNNs enable machines to "see" and interpret images
- **NLP:** traditional ML algorithms + DL transformers enable understanding and generating human language (chatbots, translation)
- **Autonomous Driving:** ML + DL process sensor data, recognize objects, make real-time decisions
- **Robotics:** reinforcement learning + DL train robots for complex tasks in dynamic environments

ML and DL fuel AI's ability to learn, adapt, and evolve. The synergy between these fields drives progress and unlocks innovation across domains.
